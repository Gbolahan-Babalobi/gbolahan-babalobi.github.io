Sheet 05 — Case study

Netflix movies of the 1990s

A production company that specializes in nostalgic styles wants to understand 1990s movies. I explored a catalogue of 4,812 Netflix titles to find the most common runtime of the decade and to count its short action movies.

ToolsPython, pandas, matplotlib
Datanetflix_data.csv, 4,812 titles
SourceDataCamp project
SkillsExploratory analysis, histograms, conditional counts

What I found

183

movies from the 1990s in the catalogue, out of 4,677 movies in total.

95–111 min

is where runtimes cluster: the tallest bar of the histogram holds 47 films. The single most common runtime is 94 minutes.

7

of the 48 action movies from the decade run for under 90 minutes.

How I approached it

  • Filtered the titles to movies, then to release years from 1990 to 1999.
  • Plotted a histogram of runtimes with matplotlib to see where films cluster, then computed the most common runtime.
  • Looped over the decade’s action movies and counted those under 90 minutes.

Filter the 1990s movies, plot runtimes and count short action films
# Subset the DataFrame for type "Movie"
netflix_subset = netflix_df[netflix_df["type"] == "Movie"]

# Filter movies released in the 1990s
movies_1990s = netflix_subset[(netflix_subset['release_year'] >= 1990) & (netflix_subset['release_year'] < 2000)]

# Plot histogram of durations
plt.hist(movies_1990s['duration'])
plt.title('Histogram of Movie Durations (1990s)')
plt.xlabel('Duration (minutes)')
plt.ylabel('Number of Movies')
plt.show()

# The histogram peaks at roughly 95-111 minutes; the exact most common runtime is computed
duration = int(movies_1990s['duration'].mode()[0])

# Filter action movies 
action_movies = movies_1990s[movies_1990s['genre'] == 'Action']

# Initialize a counter
short_movie_count = 0

for lab, row in action_movies.iterrows():
    if row['duration'] < 90:
        short_movie_count += 1

print(f"most frequent duration is {duration}")
print(f"short_movie_count is {short_movie_count}")
