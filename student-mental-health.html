Sheet 05 — Case study

International students and mental health

A Japanese international university surveyed its students in 2018, and the published study tied social connectedness and acculturative stress to depression among international students. Using PostgreSQL, I explored the survey data to see whether length of stay is part of the picture.

ToolsSQL, PostgreSQL
Datastudents table, 286 respondents, 50 columns
SourceDataCamp project
SkillsFiltering, grouping, averages, reading small samples

What I found

201

international students among the 286 survey respondents.

7.48 → 9.09

average depression (PHQ-9) score between one and three years of stay. It is 8.57 at four years.

72.8 → 87.7

average acculturative stress score between one and four years of stay, while social connectedness slips from 38.1 to 33.9.

Only seven international students stayed longer than four years, with at most three in any group, so I read the one-to-four-year rows only. These are group averages from a small survey: they show a pattern, not a cause.

How I approached it

  • Previewed the students table (286 rows, 50 columns) to find the depression, social connectedness and acculturative stress fields.
  • Filtered to international students and grouped them by length of stay.
  • Calculated the student count and the average of each score for every group, rounded to two decimals.

Compare scores by length of stay
SELECT
	stay,
	COUNT(*) AS count_int,
	ROUND(AVG(todep), 2) AS average_phq,
	ROUND(AVG(tosc), 2) AS average_scs,
	ROUND(AVG(toas), 2) AS average_as
FROM students
WHERE inter_dom = 'Inter'
GROUP BY stay
ORDER BY stay DESC
