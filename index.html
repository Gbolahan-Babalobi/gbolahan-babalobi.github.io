Sheet 01 — About

From blueprints to dashboards

I trained as a civil engineer — B.Eng, Civil Engineering, Covenant University (2019–2025) — and spent years thinking in tolerances, structures, and precision. Somewhere along the way, I found myself pulled toward a different kind of building: building with data.

Over the past two years I've deliberately re-trained as a data professional — certifications in SQL and Excel through DataCamp, a Data Science certification through ALX Africa / Explore AI Academy, and Python and applied data science through WorldQuant University's Applied Data Science Lab. I've since worked hands-on as a video data annotator for Turing, helping prepare footage used to train AI models.

What ties it together isn't a sudden pivot — it's a consistent thread. I want to work with structure, precision, and systems, whether that's a building or a dataset. Right now, that means two things I do well: turning raw data into dashboards people can act on, and doing the careful, detail-heavy work of annotating and training data for AI systems.
