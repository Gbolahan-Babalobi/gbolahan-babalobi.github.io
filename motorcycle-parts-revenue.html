Motorcycle parts: wholesale net revenue

A motorcycle-parts seller runs three warehouses and accepts card, cash and bank transfer, each with its own fee. Its board wanted wholesale revenue by product line, and how it varies by month and warehouse. I wrote one query that returns net revenue after payment fees.

ToolsSQL, PostgreSQL
Datasales table, orders from June to August 2021
SourceDataCamp project
SkillsConditional logic, aggregation, multi-level grouping

What I found

$159,640

total wholesale net revenue from June to August 2021.

Frame & body

is the largest product line at $39,478, just ahead of Suspension & traction at $38,295. Miscellaneous is the smallest at $15,748.

49%

of net revenue came through the Central warehouse ($78,856), against 36% for North and 14% for West.

August

is the strongest month at $61,456, roughly $12,000 above June or July.

Totals above are sums of the 48 rows the query returns.

How I approached it

  • Kept only Wholesale orders.
  • Turned the order date into month names (June, July, August) with CASE and EXTRACT.
  • Calculated net revenue as the sum of total minus the sum of payment_fee, grouped by product line, month and warehouse, and sorted the results.

The code and output

Taken from my DataCamp Workspace notebook for this project.

Net revenue by product line, month and warehouse
SELECT
	product_line,
	CASE WHEN EXTRACT('month' from date) = 6 THEN 'June'
        WHEN EXTRACT('month' from date) = 7 THEN 'July'
        WHEN EXTRACT('month' from date) = 8 THEN 'August'
    END as month,
	warehouse,
	SUM(total) - SUM(payment_fee) AS net_revenue
FROM sales
WHERE client_type = 'Wholesale'
GROUP BY product_line, month, warehouse
ORDER BY product_line, month, net_revenue DESC;
